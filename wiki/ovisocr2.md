# OvisOCR2

OvisOCR2 is an end-to-end 0.8B document parser produced by post-training Qwen3.5-0.8B. Given a page image, it emits Markdown in reading order, covering text, formulas, tables, and visual regions. The reported training combines filtered parser-derived real data with source-aligned synthetic pages, then SFT, RL on a 4B branch, on-policy distillation (OPD) into the 0.8B branch, and weighted parameter fusion.[^ovisocr2-report]

## Data engine

For real pages, the authors run PaddleOCR-VL-1.5 or MinerU2.5-Pro, parse each structured JSON output rather than retaining its raw Markdown, and normalize the result into one schema. Rules validate categories, normalize text and math delimiters, require valid HTML tables, represent visual regions as normalized-coordinate HTML image tags, and reject selected malformed, empty, duplicate, or repetitive outputs. They then manually spot-check random image--Markdown pairs within each source/parser/configuration subset and exclude subsets with frequent errors; the report does not quantify the sampling rate or label-error rate.[^ovisocr2-report]

For synthetic data, hard in-the-wild and self-test failures seed HTML templates generated by a multimodal model. An agent diversifies the templates while preserving their structural intent; Markdown and page pixels are independently produced from the same HTML source, with browser-rendered DOM geometry supplying visual-region coordinates. The authors preview generated batches, revise templates or rendering rules, then filter invalid, degenerate, localization-failing, or duplicate pairs. This is a reported instance of [DOM-based document synthesis](dom-based-document-synthesis.md), with hard samples directing the template families rather than a claim that the reported generator eliminates real-document label noise.[^ovisocr2-report]

## Training

Both Qwen3.5-0.8B and Qwen3.5-4B receive full-parameter SFT with a 16K maximum sequence length and a dynamic image-resolution budget. The report trains the 0.8B branch for two epochs and the 4B branch for 20% of an epoch; the latter then receives GRPO RL on principally synthetic hard cases plus a smaller high-quality real subset.[^ovisocr2-report]

The RL reward averages only components available in the reference: text similarity as one minus normalized edit distance, formula CDM, and table TEDS. Max-length truncation and unparseable structures zero the affected components. On-policy filtering emphasizes examples with outcome variation, excluding very easy, extremely hard, and reward-flat candidates.[^ovisocr2-report]

The 4B RL policy supervises 0.8B student rollouts through top-k reverse-KL distribution matching on states actually visited by the student. The stated support restriction reduces the distribution tensor from $O(TV)$ to $O(Tk)$ for response length $T$, vocabulary size $V$, and top-k support $k$. The report's plotted direct-RL comparison associates 0.8B RL with rising KL loss and degrading validation table TEDS; it is a reported training comparison, not evidence that OPD generally dominates compact-model RL. The final model is a weighted average of multiple candidate variants.[^ovisocr2-report]

## Reported evaluation

All values are author-reported and were not independently reproduced from the local bundle:[^ovisocr2-report]

- **OmniDocBench v1.6:** **96.58** overall, 0.025 text edit distance, 97.53 formula CDM, 94.76 table TEDS, 97.16 TEDS-S, and 0.111 reading-order edit distance. Its table ranks it above the listed pipeline and end-to-end systems, including [PaddleOCR-VL-1.6](paddleocr-vl-1.6.md)'s reported 96.33 overall.
- **PureDocBench:** **75.06** Avg3, with 81.55, 77.09, and 66.56 on the Clean, Digital, and Real tracks. The report notes that its Real score remains below listed strong general VLMs.
- **In-house benchmark:** **85.54** overall across more than 1,000 pages. On its handwriting subset, it reports 72.28 overall; on its complex-table subset, 83.97 overall and a 0.0796 table-missing rate. The benchmark contents and implementation are not included.

## Trust limits

- The local bundle contains a technical report, bibliography, and figures, but not model weights, training code, data records, annotation outputs, prompts, evaluation code, or complete configurations. Its training procedure and performance cannot be independently reproduced here.[^ovisocr2-report]
- The real-data labels originate from two parser systems and manually screened subsets, but the report provides no source manifest, spot-check sample sizes, subset acceptance criteria, or measured residual error. The synthetic source alignment establishes consistency with rendered HTML, not realism or semantic agreement with real pages.[^ovisocr2-report]
- The public comparison table combines author-reported and leaderboard/transcribed values from systems with differing data, prompts, output formats, and pipelines. It does not establish controlled causal rankings. The in-house evaluation is especially non-auditable from this bundle.[^ovisocr2-report]
