# Macroeconomics (Krugman/Wells) — Core and Optional Course Map

This publisher-provided planning guide designates the introduction, Chapters 1–16, and selected macroeconomic chapters as **core**, while treating the graphing and policy-history material, international trade, international macroeconomics, and mathematical extensions as **optional**.[^krugman-wells-organization] These classifications are recommendations for course planning, not claims about the importance or validity of the topics.
