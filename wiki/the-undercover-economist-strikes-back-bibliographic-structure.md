# The Undercover Economist Strikes Back — bibliographic metadata and contents map

Tim Harford's *The Undercover Economist Strikes Back* (Little, Brown Book Group 2013; Riverhead Books first American edition 2014) is presented as a sequel to *The Undercover Economist* and *The Logic of Life*, with companion titles *Dear Undercover Economist* and *Adapt* also listed[^harford-frontmatter]. The frontmatter dedicates the book "To Herbie" and gives ISBN 978-1-101-61388-7 and Penguin Group publication details[^harford-frontmatter].

## Contents structure

The book is organized as an introduction plus 15 numbered chapters, followed by acknowledgments, resources, notes and index[^harford-contents]:

1. The Economy: A User's Manual
2. The Babysitting Recession
3. Money, Money, Money
4. Just Enough Inflation
5. Stimulus
6. The Prison Camp Recession
7. Output Gaps
8. The Invention of Unemployment
9. Boss-onomics
10. The Sirens of Macroeconomics
11. The Cult of GNP
12. Happynomics
13. Can Growth Continue Forever?
14. Inequality
15. The Future of Macroeconomics[^harford-contents]

Cover and title-page imagery are provided as assets but not inspected beyond file names[^harford-frontmatter].
