Heilbroner's chapter vi presents Karl Marx not primarily as revolutionary agitator — whose organizing largely failed in his lifetime — but as political economist who gave communism its claim to inevitability: capitalism, analyzed as a pure competitive system, must generate falling profitability, recurrent crises, concentration and its own gravediggers, even though Marx wrote almost nothing about the socialist future after the Day of Judgment[^heilbroner-010][^heilbroner-011].

# Synthesis

The 1848 *Communist Manifesto* offered not a moral appeal like the Utopian socialists but a philosophy of history in which proletarian victory was demonstrably inevitable; behind it lay dialectical materialism — the materialist primacy of production and exchange shaping law, government and ideas, plus the Hegelian dialectic of inherent change — and within it an economic model of surplus value and profit collapse that Heilbroner finds partly prophetic (cycles, innovation, concentration, proletarianization) and partly flawed or politically naive[^heilbroner-010][^heilbroner-011].

# 1848: spectre, revolution, and disappointment

- *Manifesto* opens: "A spectre is haunting Europe—the spectre of Communism. All the powers of old Europe have entered into a holy alliance to exorcise this spectre: Pope and Tsar, Metternich and Guizot, French Radicals and German police spies" and closes "Let the ruling classes tremble... The proletarians have nothing to lose but their chains. They have a world to win"[^heilbroner-010].
- Europe 1848: Louis Philippe abdicates to Surrey villa, Red Flag over Hôtel de Ville, Belgian king offers resignation, barricades in Berlin, riots in Italy, uprisings in Prague and Vienna — yet spontaneous, undisciplined, aimless; old order rocks back: Paris mobs subdued at cost of 10,000 casualties, National Guard, Louis Napoleon to Second Empire; Belgian king stays and abolishes assembly; Vienna/Hungary cannonaded; German constitutional assembly offers crown to Frederick William IV who refuses crown from "ignoble hands of commoners"[^heilbroner-010].
- Heilbroner's context: France "wholly without the spirit of improvement... wrought almost exclusively through the meaner and more selfish impulses" (J.S. Mill); Prussia in fourth decade 19th c. still no parliament, speech, assembly, press, jury, tolerating no deviation from divine right; Italy hodgepodge principalities; Russia under Nicholas I "cornerstone of despotism in Europe" (de Tocqueville) despite Tsar's visit to Owen's New Lanark; England by comparison "positively idyllic"[^heilbroner-010].
- Communist League's reading: 1848 only dress rehearsal; eventual success without shadow of doubt; yet authors expected less than seventy years and never glanced at Russia as incubator[^heilbroner-010].

# The men — Marx and Engels as opposites

- **Appearance and style.** Marx: dark "The Moor," flashing eyes, stocky, glowering, formidable beard, slovenly, dusty papers, eye-stinging tobacco haze. Engels: tall, fair, elegant, fencer and huntsman, swam Weser four times. Engels gay, quick, "could stutter in twenty languages," bourgeois tastes (wine), proletarian amours Mary Burns then sister Lizzie whom he romantically cast as Scottish-poet descendants; Marx ponderous, German scholar par excellence, slow, meticulous, morbidly perfectionist — Engels dashes off treatises, Marx worries one to death; Engels fazed only by Arabic's 4,000 verb roots, Marx after 20 years still speaks hideously Teutonic English ("great 'chock'") — but Marx the greater mind, Engels breadth and dash, Marx depth[^heilbroner-010].
- **Engels path.** Son of pietist Calvinist narrow-minded Rhineland manufacturer Caspar Engels; sent to Bremen to cure poetry with religion and export business; notes first-class "in mahogany ornamented with gold" versus steerage "packed like paving-stones"; converted to then-vague "communism" (rejection of private property) by age 22; Manchester textile business — façade of pleasant shops/villas hiding second Manchester of stunted population, filth, gin, evangelism, laudanum; explores every hovel; publishes *The Condition of the Working Class in England in 1844* ("most terrible verdict ever passed on industrial slums"); interlocutor's reply to "ill-built city" remark: "And yet there is a great deal of money made here; good day, sir"[^heilbroner-010].
- **Marx path.** Born 1818 Trier, second son of prosperous Jewish family soon Christianized so advocate Heinrich Marx (*Justizrat*) less restricted; Heinrich liberal, banquet clubs toasting republican Germany, rears son on Voltaire, Locke, Diderot. Universities of Bonn and Berlin: swept into Hegel debate — each idea breeds opposite, unity produces contradiction, history as flux of resolving ideas/forces, except Prussian state as "veritable earthly god." Joins Young Hegelians debating atheism/pure theoretical communism; plans philosophy career, blocked when mentor Bruno Bauer dismissed for pro-constitutional antireligious ideas; turns to journalism — *Rheinische Zeitung* editorship five months: censure for denouncing law barring peasants gathering dead wood, warning for housing editorials, suppression after insulting Tsar of Russia; moves to Paris to edit another short-lived radical journal; bourgeois intransigence coalesces into new philosophy of history; initially disapproves brash communist Engels: "I do not know communism, but a social philosophy which has as its aim the defense of the oppressed cannot be condemned so lightly"[^heilbroner-010].
- **Meeting.** Second meeting 1844 Paris, ten-day conversation, thereafter hardly a product not edited/debated together; correspondence fills volumes[^heilbroner-010].
- **Jenny von Westphalen.** Married 1843, neighbour's daughter, belle with suitors, daughter of aristocratic Privy Councillor Baron von Westphalen (humanist who talked Homer/Shakespeare and Saint-Simon despite bishop's heresy verdict); families approve — Marx social triumph, Baron humanist vindication; future includes sharing a prostitute's jail bed, begging neighbor for child's coffin money, two dismal rooms in London slum; yet deeply devoted union — Marx unkind/suspicious/wrathful to outsiders, joyous father/loving husband; daughter's scene when Jenny dying and Marx ill next door: "Never shall I forget the morning he felt himself strong enough to go into Mother's room. When they were together they were young again..." Infidelity with maid Lenchen (unpaid lifelong Westphalian servant) produced unacknowledged child[^heilbroner-010].

# Dialectical materialism

Term often called dialectical materialism — *dialectical* from Hegel's inherent change, *materialism* from grounding in social/physical environment not ideas[^heilbroner-010].

Engels, *Anti-Dühring* (against Eugen Dühring), quoted:

> "The materialist conception of history starts from the principle that production, and with production the exchange of its products, is the basis of every social order; that in every society that has appeared in history the distribution of the products, and with it the division of society into classes or estates, is determined by what is produced and how it is produced, and how the product is exchanged. According to this conception, the ultimate causes of all social changes and political revolutions are to be sought, not in the minds of men, in their increasing insight into eternal truth and justice, but in changes in the mode of production and exchange; they are to be sought not in the *philosophy* but in the *economics* of the epoch concerned."[^heilbroner-010]

- **Base and superstructure.** Every society must organize clothing/feeding/housing — pastoral, hunting, handicraft, industrial — and needs superstructure of laws, government, religion, philosophy to bind it; superstructure cannot be random but must reflect foundation: no hunting community could use industrial legal framework or vice versa[^heilbroner-010].
- **Ideas not merely passive.** Doctrine does not reduce ideas to accompaniments; ideas emanate from period and help shape next: "Men make their own history, but they do not make it just as they please; they do not make it under circumstances chosen by themselves, but under circumstances directly found, given, and transmitted from the past" (Marx on Louis Napoleon's 1852 coup)[^heilbroner-010].
- **Economic base itself moves.** Isolated medieval markets lock fingers via exploration/unification → commercial world; hand-mill replaced by steam-mill via invention → factory. "The hand-mill gives you society with the feudal lord; the steam-mill, society with the industrial capitalist"[^heilbroner-010].
- **Class struggle.** Society aggregates into classes in common relation — favorable or otherwise — to existing production; technical change rearranges them: factories destroy handicraft, new merchant class, industrial proletariat; classes whose positions jeopardized fight those enhanced — feudal lord vs merchant, guild master vs young capitalist — but history pays no attention; gradually division of wealth altered; history is pageant of ceaseless struggle to partition social wealth as technics change[^heilbroner-010].

# Capitalism's incompatibility — why revolution is inevitable on this view

Capitalism's base is integrated interdependent industrial production; its superstructure is individualistic private property appropriating output to owners of apparatus. Base needs social planning, superstructure abhors it — "Capitalism had become so complex that it needed direction, but capitalists insisted on a ruinous freedom"[^heilbroner-010].

Result:

1. Self-destruction: planless production → constant disorganization, overproducing one good while under-producing another, crises/slumps/depressions[^heilbroner-010].
2. Breeding successor: factories create technical base for rationally planned socialism and disciplined agent — embittered proletariat[^heilbroner-010].

*Manifesto* somber proclamation: "The development of modern industry... cuts from under its feet the very foundation on which the bourgeoisie produces and appropriates products. What the bourgeoisie therefore produces, above all, are its own gravediggers. Its fall and the victory of the proletariat are equally inevitable"[^heilbroner-010].

# Exile and London years — the making of *Das Kapital*

- Marx expelled from Paris at Prussian behest, married, Brussels until 1848 (*Manifesto* composed there), then briefly Germany editing newspaper printed last edition in red before suppression, then London from 1849[^heilbroner-010].
- Financial shape desperate; Engels in Manchester double life (respected on Stock Exchange) supplies stream of checks/loans; Marx financially disorderly — children have music lessons while family without heat; British Museum 10 a.m.–7 p.m. daily; writes for Charles A. Dana's *New York Tribune* (Fourierist editor) — Engels ghosts many pieces while Marx advises "You must your war-articles colour a little more"; fails to get railway clerk job for atrocious handwriting; pawns coat/shoes (must sit home), lacks postage, suffers painful boils — "I hope the bourgeoisie as long as they live will have cause to remember my carbuncles" while writing Working Day chapter; writes Engels: "My wife is ill. Little Jenny is ill. Lenchen has a sort of nervous fever and I can't call in the doctor because I have no money... For about eight or ten days we have all been living on bread and potatoes... I have written nothing for Dana because I didn't have a penny to go and read the papers... How am I to get out of this infernal mess? Finally... I have... touched some German types for a few shillings and pence"[^heilbroner-010].
- Easier only late: small bequest, some travel; Engels inheritance 1869 leaves business, meets Marx's daughter "swinging his stick in the air and singing, his face beaming"; Jenny dies 1881 after burying two of five children including only son; Marx too ill for funeral — Engels: "The Moor is dead, too" — lingers two years, disapproves daughters' husbands, weary of movement bickering: "I am not a Marxist," then quiet death March afternoon[^heilbroner-010].

# Building a movement — and intolerance

- Maxim: "The philosophers hitherto have only interpreted the world in various ways; the thing, however, is to change it" — Marx and Engels steer proletariat to maximize leverage[^heilbroner-010].
- Communist League (at *Manifesto*) — paper organization, *Manifesto* not even publicly sold, dies with 1848[^heilbroner-010].
- International Workingmen's Association (First International) 1864 — 7 million members, hand in Continental strike wave, fearsome reputation, but motley crew of Owenists, Proudhonists, Fourierists, lukewarm Socialists, rabid nationalists, trade-unionists leery of theory; Marx holds together five years, then split — followers of Bakunin (giant, Siberian exile, oratory so moving listeners would cut throats if asked) vs national turn; last meeting 1874 New York, lugubrious failure; to be followed by mild Second (Shaw, MacDonald, Pilsudski, plus Lenin and Mussolini) and infamous Third under Moscow[^heilbroner-010].
- Tone of intolerance: Marx scurrilous — stoops to anti-Semitism, "louts," "rascals," "bedbugs"[^heilbroner-010].
  - Tailor Weitling (scars from Prussian irons, valiant German worker): seeks talk of justice/brotherhood, gets merciless scientific cross-examination, confused answers, Marx striding: "Ignorance has never helped anybody yet" — audience over[^heilbroner-010].
  - Ex-Prussian captain Willich (fought German revolution, later Union general): dropped for "unMarxist" idea that "pure will" could drive revolution vs "actual conditions" — Lenin later to prove not far-fetched[^heilbroner-010].
  - Proudhon (barrelmaker's son, *What Is Property?* — Property is Theft, not all property, brilliant self-educated): asked to join, replies presciently: "Let us together seek, if you wish, the laws of society... but, for God's sake, after having demolished all the *a priori* dogmatisms, do not let us in our turn dream of indoctrinating the people.... I applaud with all my heart your thought of inviting all shades of opinion; let us carry on a good and loyal polemic... but let us not... make ourselves into the leaders of a new intolerance... let us not pose as the apostles of a new religion, even if it be the religion of logic... Let us gather together and encourage all dissent, let us outlaw all exclusiveness... let us never regard a question as exhausted... On these conditions, I will gladly enter... Otherwise, no!" Marx answers by annihilating Proudhon's *Philosophy of Poverty* with *The Poverty of Philosophy*[^heilbroner-010].
- Pattern never disappears, degenerates later into witch-hunts for deviationists/counterrevolutionaries[^heilbroner-010].

# *Das Kapital* — the model of pure capitalism

Eighteen years in process: 1851 "in five weeks," 1859 "in six weeks," 1865 "done" huge illegible manuscripts two years to edit Volume I; Marx dies 1883 with three volumes remaining — Engels Volume II 1885, III 1894, final (fourth) 1910; 2,500 pages mixing meticulous technical/mathematical exhaustion with passion — capital's "vampire thirst for the living blood of labour," coming into world "dripping from head to foot, from every pore, with blood and dirt"[^heilbroner-010][^heilbroner-011].

Method: eschew moralizing catalog of defects, erect most rigorous pure capitalism imaginable — no monopolies, no unions, no special advantages — to seek intrinsic laws of motion; if best capitalism heads for disaster, real capitalism follows faster[^heilbroner-010][^heilbroner-011].

## Value and the profit paradox

World of perfect capitalism: every commodity sells at proper price — its *value* (essentially following Ricardo): amount of labor embodied — hats twice shoes if twice labor; direct, overhead, machine-passed labor all reducible to labor; price equals labor content[^heilbroner-010].

Protagonists redefined:

- Worker: free bargaining agent selling sole commodity — labor power — not slave to reproduction; rise not squandered in proliferation[^heilbroner-010].
- Capitalist: not mere rapacity but owner-entrepreneur in endless race; must accumulate or be accumulated[^heilbroner-010].

Paradox: if everything exchanges at exact value, how can system-wide profit exist without someone gouging (which merely shifts loss elsewhere)? Marx answers via commodity unlike others: labor power[^heilbroner-010].

Labor power sells at its value: labor to "make" labor-power — socially necessary labor to keep laborer alive. Worth e.g. six hours/day maintenance → $6/day at $1/hour. But laborer contracts for full 8-11 hour day (in Marx's time 10-11, Manchester factory 1862: 84h week for month and a half, prior 18 months 78.5h) — produces 10-11 hours value, paid for six. Unpaid layer is **surplus value** — not moral indignation phrase: worker entitled only to value of labor-power, gets it, but capitalist gets full working-day value longer than paid hours; thus can sell products at true value and still profit[^heilbroner-010][^heilbroner-011].

Why workers accept? Capitalists monopolize access to means of production — own jobs via machines/equipment; unwilling to work demanded hours → no job; system "equitable" yet all workers "cheated," forced longer than self-sustenance demands[^heilbroner-011].

## Dynamics — how profit destroys itself

1. All capitalists profit, but compete to expand; need more laborers, bid wages up, surplus value falls — echoing Smith/Ricardo profit squeeze[^heilbroner-011].
2. Smith/Ricardo rescue via Malthusian population growth; Marx brands Malthus "libel on the human race" (proletariat cannot dissipate gains via appetite) and offers different rescue: laborsaving machinery throwing part of workforce into **Industrial Reserve Army**, competing wages back to subsistence-value[^heilbroner-011].
3. Twist: substituting machines for men substitutes nonprofitable for profitable means. In pure model no profit by sharp bargaining — machine worth $10,000 over life costs full $10,000; profit only from living labor's unpaid hours. So reducing proportion of workers kills golden goose — yet each capitalist *must*: wages rising, competitor will mechanize; must narrow surplus base to cut costs and stay ahead[^heilbroner-011].
4. Spiral: profits shrink → redouble mechanization to get step ahead → ratio of living labor (surplus value) to total output shrinks further → rate of profit falls and falls → point where production no longer profitable; consumption dwindles as machines displace men, employed fails to keep pace with output; bankruptcies, scramble to dump goods, smaller firms go under — crisis[^heilbroner-011].
5. Crisis as system working: workers accept subvalue wages, stronger capitalists acquire dumped machinery below true value, surplus value reappears, march resumes — business slump/recession in modern terms[^heilbroner-011].
6. But each renewal same ending worse: competition → higher wages → labor-displacing machinery → smaller surplus base → more frenzied competition → crisis *worse than preceding* — bigger firms absorb smaller in each slump, so when monsters go down wreckage greater[^heilbroner-011].
7. Finale — Marx's Damnation passage:

> "Along with the constantly diminishing number of the magnates of capital, who usurp and monopolize all advantages of this process of transformation, grows the mass of misery, oppression, slavery, degradation, exploitation; but with this too grows the revolt of the working-class, a class always increasing in numbers, and disciplined, united, organized by the very mechanism of the process of capitalist production itself.... Centralization of the means of production and socialization of labour at last reach a point where they become incompatible with their capitalist integument. This integument bursts asunder. The knell of capitalist private property sounds. The expropriators are expropriated."[^heilbroner-011]

Thus pure system breaks down squeezing out its own energy source, hastened by planless instability; forces prolong end but final death struggle inescapable[^heilbroner-011].

Successor: "classless" — economic property division removed once society owns means of production — but "how society would 'own' factories; what was meant by 'society'; whether bitterness between managers/managed, political chieftains/rank and file" — none discussed; transitional "dictatorship of the proletariat" then "pure" communism; Marx not architect — that task falls to Lenin; *Capital* is Doomsday Book, almost nothing beyond Judgment Day[^heilbroner-011].

# Assessment — what holds, what fails

## The easy dismissal and its limits

Critics seize on value→price transition: model built on labor values must mirror dollars and cents, but in that transition Marx lands in "most terrible tangle of mathematics" and makes mistake. Mistake not irreparable — through worse tangle equations can be made "right" — but critics took "wrong" as final, and when rectified no one attended; beyond math remain hard questions: can surplus value apply under monopoly/scientific technology? Has "labor" as measuring rod been disposed of? Many non-Marxist economists thus toss scheme as awkward/inflexible[^heilbroner-011].

Yet Heilbroner insists two extraordinary properties remain:

### 1. Invention of the critique of economics — socio-analysis

Earlier economists asked how prices reflect labor-time (Smith salmon/deer, Ricardo). Marx says not perplexing question. Real question: how can "labor" be common denominator when labors so different (no deer killed with fishing rod, no salmon with hunter)? Answer: capitalist society creates *abstract labor* — detached from personal attributes, buyable/sellable like wheat/coal. Insight is not price determination but *identification of kind of social system in which labor-power becomes commodity* — capitalism where historical forces (enclosure etc.) created propertyless class with no alternative but to sell sheer ability to work[^heilbroner-011].

### 2. Extraordinary predictive "laws of motion" — representative figure is a process

Given assumptions, situation *changed* foreseeably: falling profits, quest for new techniques, cycles, concentration, proletarianization. Representative figure not labor leader or hero but dialectical process itself[^heilbroner-011].

Check against history:

- Profits do tend to fall in competitive phases — not original nor only for Marx's reason, but Smith/Ricardo/Mill and businessmen confirm competitive/wage pressures cut profits; no business permanently maintains price much above cost; profits perpetuated only by growth[^heilbroner-011].
- Need for growth → ceaseless innovation: not mere accompaniment but vital ingredient; business must innovate or die; example: large chemical firm 3/4 income from products unknown 10 years ago — exceptionally inventive but relationship generally holds[^heilbroner-011].
- Three more predictions vindicated despite daring in 1867 when bigness exception and small enterprise ruled: business crises/cycles (no other economist then recognized as inherent — events since vindicated), giant enterprise dominance, absorption of small independents — US self-employed 3/4 early 19th c. → ~10% today; office/bus driver/bank teller are proletarians in Marx's sense (must offer labor-power) vs farmer/cobbler who own means[^heilbroner-011].
- But not exact: Marx predicted not just cyclical profit fall (which occurs) but long secular downward trend — does not appear to have taken place[^heilbroner-011].

## Why real breakdown was social-political, not physically inevitable

Heilbroner stresses European breakdown *did* occur for Marx-foreseen reason: capitalism broke down — Russia/Eastern Europe displaced by socialism, Germany/Italy drifted into fascism; wars/political power/revolutionaries contributed but underlying instability as Marx said; succession of worsening crises compounded by wars destroyed faith of lower/middle classes[^heilbroner-011].

Yet difficulties not physically insuperable — antimonopoly or antibusiness-cycle policies not inconceivable, merely *politically* impossible in Marx's view because state is political ruling organ of economic rulers, not neutral referee; assuming government could rise above class interest assumed men could free from immediate self-interest shackles — Marx doubted. Lack of social flexibility, bondage to shortsighted interest, weakened Europe until after WWII: governments unconsciously vindicating prophecy by obstinately doing what he said they would — Russia stamping out democratic trade unionism, England/Germany encouraging monopolies/cartels, enormous rich-poor gulf with total indifference, psychological stereotypes too truly drawn[^heilbroner-011].

America different: also exploitation/ugliness, but land untouched by aristocratic lineage/class attitudes; pragmatism with power and general subscription to democracy steered past rocks — though clung to rugged individualism long after individual overwhelmed by massive industrialism, while Europe had *noblesse oblige* alongside class divisions[^heilbroner-011].

Modern lesson: problems diagnosed — instability, concentration of wealth/power — still with us, including worsening crises; but responses differ widely across nations: despite higher unemployment, many European countries provide free universal education (including college), health/pension/unemployment benefits on scales putting US to shame — US poverty proportion 3–4× theirs. Point in weighing Marx: failure to allow for sociopolitical culture — barely mentioned — spectrum of views on prerogatives of capital, centrality of market, private vs public roles among capitalist institutions; successor vision must be sought in that spectrum[^heilbroner-011].

Shorn of inevitable doom, analysis "remains gravest, most penetrating examination capitalist system has ever undergone... not along moral lines... of Marxist revolutionary but of Marxist economist... for all its passion, a dispassionate appraisal" and pertinent[^heilbroner-011].

## Marx as explorer

Engels graveside oration: "just as Darwin discovered the law of evolution in organic nature, so Marx discovered the law of evolution in human history" — too much, but emphasizes importance of vision of historic process as arena where social classes struggle for supremacy. Like Freud teaching to look *through* personality façade to psychic processes, or Plato to look through unexamined ideas to veiled philosophy, Marx taught to look *through* history. Not infallible despite idol worship, but *unavoidable* — great explorer whose footprints indelibly imprinted continent of social thought he discovered; all further explorers must pay respects[^heilbroner-011].

Heilbroner also notes broader framing: at least until Soviet debacle Marx widely considered religious leader ranking with Christ/Mohammed, Engels as Saint Paul/John; Moscow Marx-Engels Institute scholars idolatry they ridiculed in antireligious museums; elsewhere regarded as creature of devil; merits neither — belongs in great line of economic viewpoints, not without flaw; world preoccupied with Marx Revolutionary, but had he not lived other Socialists/prophets would have come; final imprint is prediction capitalism must inevitably collapse — on which communism built edifice, heedless of its own weaknesses[^heilbroner-010].

# Contradictions

- Orthodox dismissal that Marx's value-price transformation error proves whole system "wrong" vs later mathematical rectifications making equations "right" but ignored — Heilbroner presents as sociological reception, not logical resolution; deeper awkwardness of labor-as-measure and surplus value under monopoly/technology remains agitated among scholars[^heilbroner-011].
- Heilbroner's own assessment: extraordinary predictive power (cycle, innovation, concentration, proletarianization) vs failure of secular profit fall and fatal breakdown in Anglo-American capitalism where sociopolitical flexibility allowed adaptation — tension resolved not by refuting economics but by adding cultural-political variable Marx underplayed[^heilbroner-011].
- Historical expectation vs outcome: 1848 dress-rehearsal inevitability and 70-year wait vs actual Russian incubation ignored, and breakdown via social faith loss rather than pure physical profit exhaustion — Heilbroner notes physical cure was not inconceivable, political will was[^heilbroner-010][^heilbroner-011].
- Intolerance as inheritance: Proudhon's plea for dissent and never regarding question exhausted vs Marxist institutionalized intolerance and witch-hunts — presented as prophetic continuity without resolving normative claim[^heilbroner-010].
