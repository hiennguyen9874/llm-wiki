# LLM Wiki

The complete retrieval map for compiled knowledge. See [LLM Wiki Contract](../AGENTS.md) for storage and maintenance rules.

## Concepts

- [Browser-based weight-space editing](browser-weight-space-editing.md) — A reported WebGPU demo edits and compares language-model weights locally, including embedding-direction injection and model blending.
- [Clinical decision network weight audit](clinical-decision-network-weight-audit.md) — A synthetic radiotherapy study reports weight-based flaw detection and sparse repairs, alongside testing and numerical-safety limits.
- [Coarse normalization-weight editing experiment](coarse-normalization-weight-editing-experiment.md) — A reported code-repair experiment illustrates silent no-op edits, noisy baselines, and failure after coarse normalization gains.
- [Continual learning and recursive improvement](continual-learning-and-recursive-improvement.md) — Conditions for retained capabilities across model updates and for improvement processes to improve across generations.
- [Dataset-prior audit of neural weight reading](weight-reading-dataset-prior-audit.md) — An exploratory CNN study separates local weight statistics from potentially prior-driven dataset and class naming.
- [Italian translation weight-edit experiment](italian-translation-weight-edit-experiment.md) — A reported sparse Gemma weight edit targets an Italian tense preference while comparing related and unrelated translation side effects.
- [LLM-directed neural weight editing](llm-directed-neural-weight-editing.md) — Reported neural policy construction, classification, and model repair by directly changing weights.
- [Model adaptation mechanisms](model-adaptation-mechanisms.md) — A comparison of direct weight editing, fine-tuning, distillation, prompt baking, and retrieval by how they change model behavior.
- [Reading neural network weights with LLMs](reading-neural-network-weights-with-llms.md) — Reported experiments using an LLM to infer learned behavior and circuits from weights, sometimes with restricted activation evidence.
- [Weight-space programming](weight-space-programming.md) — Reasoning-driven inspection and direct editing of neural network weights by a general-purpose language model.
- [Writing Weights Index](writing-weights-index.md) — A reported three-game index comparing language models on direct neural weight writing against PPO and random baselines.
