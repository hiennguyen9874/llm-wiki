# Macroeconomics (Blanchard 8th Edition) — Structure and Course Map

Blanchard's *Macroeconomics* 8th edition starts with two introductory chapters, then organizes its core around the short, medium, and long runs; extensions cover expectations and the open economy; three policy chapters and a historical epilogue complete the text.[^blanchard-brief-contents] The preface presents this as an integrated framework built around equilibrium in goods, financial, and labor markets, simplifying or expanding components as the question requires.[^blanchard-brief-contents]

## Course-planning guidance in the preface

- The text says Chapters 3–13 constitute the core. A short course of 15 lectures or fewer can cover the two introductory chapters and core while omitting Chapter 13 without disrupting continuity; informal coverage of expectations (Chapter 16) and the open economy (Chapter 17) then yields 14 lectures.[^blanchard-brief-contents]
- An alternative short course omits long-run growth and uses Chapters 1–9, then Chapters 16–19, for 13 lectures.[^blanchard-brief-contents]
- For a 20–25 lecture course, the preface recommends the core plus either or both extensions and the policy review; extensions assume the core but are otherwise mostly self-contained. It says teaching expectations before the open economy helps students understand interest parity and exchange-rate crises.[^blanchard-brief-contents]
