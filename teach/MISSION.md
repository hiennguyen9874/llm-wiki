# Mission: LLM Model Architecture

## Why
Develop practical understanding of how LLM model architectures work, so I can reason about their optimization and deployment trade-offs and read architecture papers critically. I am focusing on model architecture rather than training models.

## Success looks like
- Trace a modern causal LLM’s data flow and explain each major component’s purpose and trade-offs.
- Identify what an architecture paper changes relative to a dense causal-Transformer baseline and evaluate its evidence.
- Explain architecture-relevant optimization and deployment choices, including inference-state and latency trade-offs.

## Constraints
- No model-training projects; use conceptual, paper-reading, and lightweight inspection exercises.
- Advanced Python; basic linear algebra and calculus.
- Weekly study time has not yet been specified.

## Out of scope
- Training a model end to end.
- Data curation, post-training alignment, and agent scaffolding except where needed to interpret an architecture paper.
