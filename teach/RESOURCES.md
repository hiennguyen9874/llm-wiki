# LLM Model Architecture Resources

## Knowledge

- [Paper: “Attention Is All You Need” — Vaswani et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762)
  Primary source for the Transformer’s attention, position-wise feed-forward, residual, normalization, and positional-encoding components. Use for: the dense baseline.
- [Paper: “Improving Language Understanding by Generative Pre-Training” — Radford et al.](../raw/gpt.pdf)
  Primary source bundled with this workspace for a decoder-only causal language model and BPE vocabulary. Use for: GPT-style causal architecture and next-token pre-training framing.
- [Paper: “Scaling Laws for Neural Language Models” — Kaplan et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.08361)
  Primary empirical source on cross-entropy, model/data/compute scaling in the reported decoder-only Transformer setting. Use for: interpreting scaling claims and their limits.
