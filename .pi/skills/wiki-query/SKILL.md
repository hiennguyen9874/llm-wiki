# Wiki Query

Retrieve progressively: state first, map second, concepts third, raw evidence only under policy.

## Steps

1. From the repository root, read the retrieval policy in `LLM-WIKI.md`, inspect the reserved files, and independently enumerate concept files enough to classify the wiki state. Report an uninitialized or valid empty wiki as having no compiled knowledge; absence is not disproof and does not trigger an automatic `raw/` search. For a damaged wiki, disclose the integrity issue, enumerate concepts independently, and route repair through `wiki-lint` rather than presenting retrieval as complete. For a usable inventory, translate the question into concepts, aliases, relationships, source coverage, and constraints. This step is complete when the state, candidate vocabulary, and known retrieval limits are explicit.

2. Build a candidate union progressively. Read `wiki/index.md` and applicable group indexes; use glob for structural scope and exact text search for titles, aliases, tags, identifiers, metadata, source identities, and relationship labels. Include relevant `SourceMap` pages for coverage or completeness questions. When exact retrieval is too broad or misses the user's vocabulary, follow `qmd-retrieval`: use BM25 `search` for lexical ranking and hybrid `query` only for semantic, ambiguous, or cross-concept gaps. QMD is optional; an unavailable or stale cache falls back to repository search, and its scores cannot discard catalog or exact matches. This step is complete when paths from every applicable channel are deduplicated and every remaining candidate gap is explicit.

3. Read candidate concepts and traverse relationship types suited to the question: `Depends on` and `Uses` for impact, `Caused` and `Fixed by` for diagnosis, `Supersedes` and `Contradicts` for freshness, and `Owned by` for responsibility. For large pages, read metadata, synthesis, headings, coverage limits, contradictions, and relationships first, then only relevant sections; prefer linked cohesive concepts over treating a source-shaped overview as exhaustive. Respect uninspected attachments, code paths, and other recorded limits. This step is complete when every relevant relationship frontier and coverage boundary has been checked.

4. Evaluate lifecycle, freshness, contradictions, and provenance before synthesis. For each consequential claim, distinguish reported evidence, static observation, reproduced evidence, synthesis, independent verification, and unresolved uncertainty. Check matching source IDs, footnote definitions, resolvable resources, and useful locators. Open raw evidence only for a disputed citation, a broken or too-coarse provenance edge, inconsistent wiki summaries, or explicit source-level research such as exact implementation, equation, benchmark, or wording. Cite the exact file and best available section, figure, table, symbol, key, region, or timestamp when raw material is used. This step is complete when every material answer claim is supported, identified as synthesis, or marked uncertain and every provenance limitation is visible.

5. Answer directly with Markdown links to wiki concepts. Distinguish: documented compiled knowledge; synthesis from documented concepts; related but insufficient evidence; no matching compiled knowledge; and retrieval limited by an empty, uninitialized, or damaged wiki. State whether raw research was performed. This step is complete when the requested scope is answered and every material claim has a visible basis without turning missing knowledge into a negative factual claim.

6. Crystallize through `wiki-ingest` only when the result creates reusable multi-concept synthesis, a durable comparison, a decision or lesson, a supported relationship, a contradiction resolution, or a verified procedure. Length alone is not durable value. Cite underlying concepts or raw sources rather than transient chat and pass the result through the full ingest scope, provenance, coverage, maturity, and validation gates. Save requested transient deliverables under `outputs/`; read-only answers leave the wiki and log unchanged. This step is complete when each durable insight is filed or explicitly left transient.
