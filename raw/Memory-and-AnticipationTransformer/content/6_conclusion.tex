\section{Conclusion and Future Work}
\label{sec:conclusion}
We present Memory-and-Anticipation Transformer (MAT), a novel memory-anticipation-based paradigm for online action detection and anticipation, to overcome the weakness of most existing methods that can only complete modeling temporal dependency within a limited historical context. 
Through extensive experiments on four challenging benchmarks across two tasks, we show its applicability in predicting present or future actions, obtaining state-of-the-art results, and demonstrating the importance of circular interaction between memory and anticipation in the entire temporal structure. Although the temporal dimension is only considered in this work, 
we believe MAT would be a general paradigm for any AI system used to analyze video online and predict future events.
In the near future, we will validate our model on more benchmarks and extend it to the long-term anticipation task. In the longer term, we will continue to unearth the intrinsic association of memory with anticipation and develop more effective forecasting models.
