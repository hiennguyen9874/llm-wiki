\section{Limitations}

Despite a robust performance shown in our model, several limitations remains:

\noindent\textbf{Images with Text Overlays}. Prior research \citep{liu2024mm} indicates that integrating multiple modalities within a single image (e.g., visual elements combined with overlaid text) can create nuanced harmfulness. A visually benign image, for instance, might be rendered unsafe by the specific meaning of text embedded within the image itself. It is beyond the scope of our detector for this specific challenge of evaluating unsafe content that emerges pragmatically from the interplay of different modalities co-existing within one image.

\noindent\textbf{Interleaving Conversation}. A limitation of our model is its focus on single-image classification. It is not designed for, and therefore beyond the scope of this work, to process interleaved sequences of text and images, such as those found in conversational contexts.

\noindent\textbf{Limited policy coverage}. Even though our model can be generalized into customized policies, it's not specifically fine-tuned for policies other than sexual, danger and violence. We leave that in future work to further increase our harm policy coverage.
