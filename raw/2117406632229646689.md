GPT-6 Astra can edit Qwen3.5-0.8B’s weights and raise its MATH-500 from 7.9% to 48.1%, without gradients or retraining.

“Sparks of Weight-Space Programming”

This blog from MIT asks whether LLMs can read and edit neural network weights directly, much like code.

In Qwen, Astra traced repetitive reasoning loops to a direction written by two MLP layers, then edited the weights to suppress it.

With 50 batches of forward-pass feedback, the fix also raised instruction following from 21.0% to 62.3%
