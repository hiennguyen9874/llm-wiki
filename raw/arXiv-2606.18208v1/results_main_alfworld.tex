Table~\ref{tab:alfworldmain} presents evaluation results on the AlfWorld dataset. On this dataset, we see that the trend can still be promising, as our proposed model gives a promising overall result, given the fact that it is pretty small in terms of model size, with around 1B parameters. Notably, it gives the best result on the BLEU metrics \citep{papineni-etal-2002-bleu} among four models, and ranks in second place on EM and Token F1. Further, by inspecting the detailed action categories, we found that our model seems to have low entity scores, and it seems valid for most action categories. Such an error analysis indicates that future optimization can focus on the entity scores to further enhance the model.
