Weight Space Programming: a glimpse into continual learning.

Agent edited Qwen 0.8B's weights to inject facts about my X profile directly into the model: my handle, bio, GitHub, and what i build. It edited 2% of the network (5 of 488 tensors). No training, no gradients.

Result:  
Before, it had never heard of me ("I don't have access to private information…").  
After, it tells you who i am, my bio and GitHub, answer project questions it was never trained on. Everything else it knew is untouched.
