\section{Tokenizer Training}
\label{sec:tokenizer}

We train a custom BPE tokenizer with a vocabulary size of 32,768 using the HuggingFace \texttt{tokenizers} library. We follow a GPT-4 style configuration \citep{openai2024gpt4technicalreport}: byte-level BPE with byte fallback, no text normalization, and a GPT-4 style pre-tokenization split pattern. The tokenizer is trained on 2 billion characters from the FineWeb-Edu training set \citep{penedo2024finewebdatasetsdecantingweb}, with individual documents capped at 10,000 characters. We define three special tokens: \texttt{<|bos|>}, \texttt{<|eos|>}, and \texttt{<|pad|>}. A small comparison of our tokenizer used in our experiments with others can be found in \cref{tab:tokenizer-compression}.
