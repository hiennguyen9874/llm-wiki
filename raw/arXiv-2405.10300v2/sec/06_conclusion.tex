\section{Conclusion}
This paper has presented Grounding DINO 1.5, a series of models to advance the field of open-set object detection. 
The flagship model, Grounding DINO 1.5 Pro, has established new records on the COCO and LVIS zero-shot benchmarks, signifying a major stride in detection accuracy and reliability. 
Moreover, the Grounding DINO 1.5 Edge model enables real-time object detection across various applications, further expanding the practical utility of the Grounding DINO 1.5 series.
