# Organization of This Book

To help plan your course, we’ve listed what we consider to be core and optional chapters, with descriptions about the coverage in each.

## Optional

- <div id="kru_9781319245269_FM_acknowledgments1.xhtml#pau_9781319320164_vmqqbfoXju">

  <a href="#kru_9781319245269_ch00_01.xhtml" id="kru_9781319245269_FM_acknowledgments1.xhtml#pau_9781319320164_d3Wn124xhx" class="crossref"><strong>Introduction: An Engine for Growth and Discovery</strong></a>

> Initiates students into the study of economics using China’s Pearl River Delta as the motivating story. Includes basic terms and explains the difference between microeconomics and macroeconomics.

## Core

- <div id="kru_9781319245269_FM_acknowledgments1.xhtml#pau_9781319320164_dhJsVo6SMt">

  <a href="#kru_9781319245269_ch01_01.xhtml" id="kru_9781319245269_FM_acknowledgments1.xhtml#pau_9781319320164_ACAohvywHx" class="crossref"><strong>1. First Principles</strong></a>

  Outlines 12 principles underlying the study of economics: principles of individual choice, interaction between individuals, and economy-wide interaction.

- <div id="kru_9781319245269_FM_acknowledgments1.xhtml#pau_9781319320164_lGBhKahULZ">

  <a href="#kru_9781319245269_ch02_01.xhtml" id="kru_9781319245269_FM_acknowledgments1.xhtml#pau_9781319320164_5BXwn1k916" class="crossref"><strong>2. Economic Models: Trade-offs and Trade</strong></a>

  Employs two economic models — the production possibilities frontier and comparative advantage — as an introduction to gains from trade and international comparisons. Also introduces the circular-flow diagram.

## Optional

- <div id="kru_9781319245269_FM_acknowledgments1.xhtml#pau_9781319320164_Oc2dMt7dSD">

  <a href="#kru_9781319245269_ch02_app.xhtml" id="kru_9781319245269_FM_acknowledgments1.xhtml#pau_9781319320164_zuo4hIIU5K" class="crossref"><strong>2 Appendix: Graphs in Economics</strong></a>

> A comprehensive review of graphing and math skills for students who would find this background helpful.

## Core

- <div id="kru_9781319245269_FM_acknowledgments1.xhtml#pau_9781319320164_pNNcQc1ZR9">

  <a href="#kru_9781319245269_ch03_01.xhtml" id="kru_9781319245269_FM_acknowledgments1.xhtml#pau_9781319320164_P9Wj4uD527" class="crossref"><strong>3. Supply and Demand</strong></a>

  Covers the essentials of supply, demand, market equilibrium, surplus, and shortage.

<!-- -->

- <div id="kru_9781319245269_FM_acknowledgments1.xhtml#pau_9781319320164_8wwjdu2ImV">

  <a href="#kru_9781319245269_ch04_01.xhtml" id="kru_9781319245269_FM_acknowledgments1.xhtml#pau_9781319320164_sMyyaoGkBu" class="crossref"><strong>4. Price Controls and Quotas: Meddling with Markets</strong></a>

  Covers market interventions and their consequences: price and quantity controls, inefficiency, and deadweight loss.

## Optional

- <div id="kru_9781319245269_FM_acknowledgments1.xhtml#pau_9781319320164_MqHDfzmMOM">

  <a href="#kru_9781319245269_ch05_01.xhtml" id="kru_9781319245269_FM_acknowledgments1.xhtml#pau_9781319320164_NKQTUNO2d7" class="crossref"><strong>5. International Trade</strong></a>

> An examination of comparative advantage, tariffs and quotas, the politics of trade protection and international trade agreements, and the controversy over imports from low-wage countries. With new coverage of hyperglobalization, the EU and Brexit, outsourcing, and reshoring.

- <div id="kru_9781319245269_FM_acknowledgments1.xhtml#pau_9781319320164_LGE6RRclHB">

  <a href="#kru_9781319245269_ch05_app.xhtml" id="kru_9781319245269_FM_acknowledgments1.xhtml#pau_9781319320164_MYMkttXrU0" class="crossref"><strong>5 Appendix: Consumer and Producer Surplus</strong></a>

> Introduces students to market efficiency, the ways markets fail, the role of prices as signals, and property rights.

## Core

- <div id="kru_9781319245269_FM_acknowledgments1.xhtml#pau_9781319320164_MRgeSlTyKU">

  <a href="#kru_9781319245269_ch06_01.xhtml" id="kru_9781319245269_FM_acknowledgments1.xhtml#pau_9781319320164_VlMM6OThZQ" class="crossref"><strong>6. Macroeconomics: The Big Picture</strong></a>

  Introduces the big ideas of macroeconomics with an overview of recessions and expansions, employment and unemployment, long-run growth, inflation versus deflation, and international economics.

<!-- -->

- <div id="kru_9781319245269_FM_acknowledgments1.xhtml#pau_9781319320164_2c8tUUaIc3">

  <a href="#kru_9781319245269_ch07_01.xhtml" id="kru_9781319245269_FM_acknowledgments1.xhtml#pau_9781319320164_e0vyDaDIDy" class="crossref"><strong>7. GDP and the CPI: Tracking the Macroeconomy</strong></a>

  Explains how the numbers macroeconomists use are calculated and why, including the basics of national income accounting and price indexes. Sets the stage for upcoming chapters with a newly simplified presentation of the expanded circular-flow diagram.

<!-- -->

- <div id="kru_9781319245269_FM_acknowledgments1.xhtml#pau_9781319320164_R2FekxNoZV">

  <a href="#kru_9781319245269_ch08_01.xhtml" id="kru_9781319245269_FM_acknowledgments1.xhtml#pau_9781319320164_EypsJsVyeK" class="crossref"><strong>8. Unemployment and Inflation</strong></a>

  Covers the measurement of unemployment, emphasizing that continual job creation and destruction are features of modern economies. Examines the problems inflation poses for policy makers and the economy.

<!-- -->

- <div id="kru_9781319245269_FM_acknowledgments1.xhtml#pau_9781319320164_cPJOAOwgjR">

  <a href="#kru_9781319245269_ch09_01.xhtml" id="kru_9781319245269_FM_acknowledgments1.xhtml#pau_9781319320164_cKa2TRJLOs" class="crossref"><strong>9. Long-Run Economic Growth</strong></a>

  Emphasizes an international perspective—economic growth is about the world as a whole—and explains why some countries have been more successful than others, with an updated section on sustainability.

<!-- -->

- <div id="kru_9781319245269_FM_acknowledgments1.xhtml#pau_9781319320164_Fk1rBh30W1">

  <a href="#kru_9781319245269_ch10_01.xhtml" id="kru_9781319245269_FM_acknowledgments1.xhtml#pau_9781319320164_UVWvr5hR1C" class="crossref"><strong>10. Savings, Investment Spending, and the Financial System</strong></a>

  An introduction to financial markets and institutions, loanable funds, and the determination of interest rates. Includes coverage of present value.

## Optional

- <a href="#kru_9781319245269_ch10_app.xhtml" id="kru_9781319245269_FM_acknowledgments1.xhtml#pau_9781319320164_qI7RR7jFda" class="crossref"><strong>10 Appendix: Toward a Fuller Understanding of Present Value</strong></a>

> Expands the use of present value calculations to include how firms make investment spending decisions and how investors price financial assets.

## Core

- <div id="kru_9781319245269_FM_acknowledgments1.xhtml#pau_9781319320164_nSKI93c7Af">

  <a href="#kru_9781319245269_ch11_01.xhtml" id="kru_9781319245269_FM_acknowledgments1.xhtml#pau_9781319320164_UTpVXqywbY" class="crossref"><strong>11. Income and Expenditure</strong></a>

  Addresses the determinants of consumer and investment spending, introduces the 45-degree diagram, and explains the logic of the multiplier.

## Optional

- <a href="#kru_9781319245269_ch11_app.xhtml" id="kru_9781319245269_FM_acknowledgments1.xhtml#pau_9781319320164_CTBJRgFhC3" class="crossref"><strong>11 Appendix: Deriving the Multiplier Algebraically</strong></a>

> A rigorous, mathematical approach to deriving the multiplier.

## Core

- <div id="kru_9781319245269_FM_acknowledgments1.xhtml#pau_9781319320164_bqDjHReCTK">

  <a href="#kru_9781319245269_ch12_01.xhtml" id="kru_9781319245269_FM_acknowledgments1.xhtml#pau_9781319320164_pdxLlE7Uxm" class="crossref"><strong>12. Aggregate Demand and Aggregate Supply</strong></a>

  Provides the traditional focus on aggregate price level using the traditional approach to *AD–AS.* In addition, covers the ability of the economy to recover in the long run.

<!-- -->

- <div id="kru_9781319245269_FM_acknowledgments1.xhtml#pau_9781319320164_Uuj4TuO0yj">

  <a href="#kru_9781319245269_ch13_01.xhtml" id="kru_9781319245269_FM_acknowledgments1.xhtml#pau_9781319320164_0VQCYsqrBl" class="crossref"><strong>13. Fiscal Policy</strong></a>

  Provides an analysis of the role of discretionary fiscal policy, automatic stabilizers, long-run issues of debt and solvency, and the distinction between deficit and debt—with examples of U.S. fiscal stimulus in response to the coronavirus pandemic, Great Recession, and fiscal austerity in Greece.

## Optional

- <a href="#kru_9781319245269_ch13_app.xhtml" id="kru_9781319245269_FM_acknowledgments1.xhtml#pau_9781319320164_9b3p3cwdSR" class="crossref"><strong>13 Appendix: Taxes and the Multiplier</strong></a>

> A rigorous derivation of the roles of taxes in reducing the size of the multiplier and acting as an automatic stabilizer.

## Core

- <div id="kru_9781319245269_FM_acknowledgments1.xhtml#pau_9781319320164_rpN2LX78hn">

  <a href="#kru_9781319245269_ch14_01.xhtml" id="kru_9781319245269_FM_acknowledgments1.xhtml#pau_9781319320164_QzgDYWUtZd" class="crossref"><strong>14. Money, Banking, and the Federal Reserve System</strong></a>

  Covers the roles of money, the ways in which banks create money, and the structure and the role of the Federal Reserve and other central banks. Examines the evolution of American banking with detailed coverage of banking crises. The chapter has also been enhanced with the integration of content from our once separate chapter on crises and consequences.

<!-- -->

- <div id="kru_9781319245269_FM_acknowledgments1.xhtml#pau_9781319320164_DdIDsCNBth">

  <a href="#kru_9781319245269_ch15_01.xhtml" id="kru_9781319245269_FM_acknowledgments1.xhtml#pau_9781319320164_7q8geX1A5v" class="crossref"><strong>15. Monetary Policy</strong></a>

  Covers the role of Federal Reserve policy in driving interest rates and aggregate demand. Bridges the short run and long run by showing how interest rates set in the short run reflect the supply and demand of savings in the long run.

## Optional

- <a href="#kru_9781319245269_ch15_app.xhtml" id="kru_9781319245269_FM_acknowledgments1.xhtml#pau_9781319320164_zAuFsa3jA5" class="crossref"><strong>15 Appendix: Reconciling the Two Models of the Interest Rate</strong></a>

> Explains why the loanable funds model (long-run discussions) and the liquidity preference approach (short-run discussions) are both valuable.

## Core

- <div id="kru_9781319245269_FM_acknowledgments1.xhtml#pau_9781319320164_I5fnVTOl5v">

  <a href="#kru_9781319245269_ch16_01.xhtml" id="kru_9781319245269_FM_acknowledgments1.xhtml#pau_9781319320164_y1byeU80BL" class="crossref"><strong>16. Inflation, Disinflation, and Deflation</strong></a>

  Covers the causes and consequences of inflation, the large cost deflation imposes on the economy, and the danger that disinflation leads the economy into a liquidity trap.

## Optional

- <div id="kru_9781319245269_FM_acknowledgments1.xhtml#pau_9781319320164_0jm7wNqq3P">

  <a href="#kru_9781319245269_ch17_01.xhtml" id="kru_9781319245269_FM_acknowledgments1.xhtml#pau_9781319320164_0B1CsvBnHD" class="crossref"><strong>17. Macroeconomics: Events and Ideas</strong></a>

> A unique overview of the history of macroeconomic thought, set in the context of changing policy concerns, and the current state of macroeconomic debates.

- <div id="kru_9781319245269_FM_acknowledgments1.xhtml#pau_9781319320164_e1sXkBb08J">

  <a href="#kru_9781319245269_ch18_01.xhtml" id="kru_9781319245269_FM_acknowledgments1.xhtml#pau_9781319320164_4CFFB4gAjm" class="crossref"><strong>18. International Macroeconomics</strong></a>

> Covers basic topics in international macroeconomics such as balance of payment accounts, foreign exchange markets, and exchange rates.
