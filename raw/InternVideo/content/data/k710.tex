\paragraph{Kinetics-710.} \label{k710}
We adopt a new customized kinetics action dataset Kinetics-710 \cite{uniformerv2} for supervised training, both separate and joint ones. It has 650K videos with 710 unique action labels. It combines all the unique training data from Kinetics 400/600/700~\cite{k400,k600,k700}. To avoid the training leak, some training data existing in the testing set from Kinetics of a specific version are abandoned.
