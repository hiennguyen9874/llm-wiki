\subparagraph{Kinetics\cite{k400}.} Kinetics has three versions, K400, K600, K700, based on the number of human action categories. Our Kinetics-400 has 240k training videos and 20k validation videos. Kinetics-600 has 366k training videos and 28k validation videos. Kinetics-700 has 529k training videos and 34k validation videos. We (pre-)train all models on the training set and test on the validation set.
