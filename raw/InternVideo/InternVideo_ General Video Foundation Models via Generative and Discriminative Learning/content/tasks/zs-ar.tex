\textbf{Zero-shot Action Recognition.}
Zero-shot recognition is one of the extraordinary capabilities of the original CLIP model. With our designed multimodal video encoder, we can also achieve remarkable zero-shot action recognition performance without further optimization. We evaluate our model on Kinetics-400 dataset with 64.25\% accuracy, which outperforms the previous SOTA 56.4\% \cite{Wang2021ActionCLIPAN} by a large margin.
