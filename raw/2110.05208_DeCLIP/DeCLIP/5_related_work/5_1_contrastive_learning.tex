% Contrastive learning, as a pretext task of self-supervised training, has achieved remarkable success in visual representation learning. MoCo~\citep{he2020momentum} achieves comparative learning training by building a dynamic dictionary with a queue and a moving-averaged encoder. SimCLR~\citep{chen2020simple} selects the optimal data augmentation combination method, and adds a non-linear mapping after the encoder, discards the memory bank and adopts a larger batch size to calculate the contrastive learning loss. MoCo v2~\citep{chen2020improved} draws on the idea of SimCLR~\citep{chen2020simple} and improves on the basis of MoCo~\citep{he2020momentum}. SwAV~\citep{caron2020unsupervised} clusters various types of samples during training, and then distinguishes the clusters of each type, and proposes a new data augmentation method that mixes views of different resolutions.BYOL~\citep{grill2020bootstrap} adds the Predictor module to the Projector, and learns the mapping from online encoder to target encoder through Predictor. Only consider the positive sample pairs, and use the target network stop-gradient for training. SimSiam~\citep{chen2021exploring} removes the target encoder updated by momentum on the basis of BYOL~\citep{grill2020bootstrap}. Finding that stop-gradinent is the key to avoid training collapse. SEER~\citep{goyal2021self} and DnC~\citep{tian2021divide} discards the ImageNet~\citep{deng2009imagenet} dataset commonly used for training, and use a large amount of unlabeled data crawled from the network, so that the model can learn more general knowledge from a broader open data domain. In addition, with the popularity of vision transformer, MoCo v3~\citep{chen2021empirical} applies contrastive learning to the vision transformer network, and analyzes the characteristics of the vision transformer structure when training a contrastive learning model. DINO~\citep{caron2021emerging} designs an unlabeled self-supervised method with knowledge distillation by analyzing the characteristics of ViT~\citep{dosovitskiy2020image} features. In addition to the good effects, the learned features have a strong Interpretability. 

% In the field of NLP, in addition to common pretext tasks such as Mask Language Model~\citep{devlin2018bert}, there are gradually some ideas of self-supervised training sentence-embedding using contrast learning methods. BERT-CT~\citep{carlsson2021semantic} uses two models with the same structure and different parameters to generate two views for contrastive learning to obtain higher-quality sentence representation vectors.SimCSE~\citep{gao2021simcse} is trained by randomly dropout mask in the training phase, inputting the same sample into the same encoder twice, and obtaining two different representation vectors to construct contrastive learning sample pairs for training. ConSERT~\citep{yan2021consert} proposes a contrastivelearning framework based on contrastive learning and pre-training models, which includes three parts: data enhancement, BERT coding layer, and contrast loss layer, which improves the effect of pre-training models when transfer to downstream tasks. In general, recently, contrastive learning has made significant progress in the CV and NLP fields by constructing sample pairs for training programs.
