SITUATION EXPLAINED: MIT researchers had GPT-6 Astra edit a small AI model's weights directly, and its math score jumped 6x.

• Weights are the millions to trillions of numbers that make up an AI model, and normally the only way to change them is training  
• Astra changed about 131,000 of Qwen3.5-0.8B's roughly 800 million weights, just 0.016%, with no training at all  
• Qwen's score on the MATH-500 benchmark went from 7.9% to 48.1%, and its grade-school math score from 13.8% to 67.1%  
• Astra found a pattern in 2 layers that made Qwen loop until it ran out of tokens, then deleted it  
• Given only the rules, it also wrote Breakout-playing networks from scratch that scored about 20x a standard agent trained for 500M steps  
• On a model that didn't have the looping problem, the edits barely helped, so for now it fixes bugs more than it adds skills
